Amobee (now Nexxen) is a digital advertising platform offering REST APIs for managing cross-channel programmatic campaigns, audience targeting, data management, and advertising analytics. The platform enables advertisers and agencies to plan, activate, and measure media across display, video, mobile, social, and TV channels through a unified DSP.

**Areas:** Digital Advertising · DSP · Programmatic · Campaign Management · Audience Targeting · Data Management Platform · AdTech · Samsung Ads

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Amobee: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
